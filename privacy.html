Privacy Policy

How ConceptNet collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: August 2026  ·  ConceptNet Ltd, London, UK
Summary: We collect minimal data. We never sell your personal information. The Voice IP Sandbox runs entirely in your browser — no data is sent to our servers when you use it. This policy explains everything we do collect and why.

1. Who We Are

ConceptNet Ltd is a company incorporated in England and Wales. We build voice IP infrastructure for AI — including the ConceptNet Voice IP Sandbox, benchmarks platform, and enterprise workflow automation tools.

Contact: tonymomoh@icloud.com  ·  07733 246865  ·  London, United Kingdom

2. What Data We Collect

2.1 Data you give us directly

  • Early access form — when you submit your name, email, company, and role via our website form, we store this information to contact you about ConceptNet access and updates.
  • Investment inquiries — if you contact us via the investment inquiry form or email, we retain your contact details for the purpose of responding to your inquiry.
  • Email correspondence — any email you send to tonymomoh@icloud.com is stored in our email system.

2.2 Data collected automatically

  • Website analytics — we may collect standard web analytics (page views, referrer, browser type, approximate location by country) to understand how people use our site. We do not use Google Analytics. If we use analytics software, it will be listed here.
  • GitHub — if you star, fork, or contribute to our GitHub repository (github.com/wushu75/ConceptNet), GitHub collects data per their own privacy policy.
  • Hugging Face — if you use our Hugging Face Space, Hugging Face collects data per their own privacy policy.

2.3 What we do NOT collect

  • The Voice IP Sandbox (conceptnet.co.uk/sandbox/) runs entirely in your browser. Commands you type or speak are processed locally — they are never sent to our servers.
  • We do not collect payment information (we have no paid product yet).
  • We do not collect biometric data.
  • We do not track you across other websites.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to your early access or investment inquiry
  • To send you updates about ConceptNet (only if you opted in)
  • To understand how our website and tools are used so we can improve them
  • To comply with legal obligations

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (UK GDPR)

  • Consent — when you submit the early access form, you consent to us contacting you about ConceptNet.
  • Legitimate interests — responding to inquiries and improving our product.
  • Legal obligation — where required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain your contact information for as long as it is relevant to your inquiry or our legitimate business interest. You can ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing tonymomoh@icloud.com.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Formspree — we use Formspree to handle form submissions on our website. Formspree receives and forwards your form data to us. See formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy for their policy.
  • GitHub — our open-source code is hosted on GitHub (github.com). See docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement.
  • Hugging Face — our AI Space is hosted on Hugging Face. See huggingface.co/privacy.
  • Google Fonts — our website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address. See policies.google.com/privacy.

7. Cookies

Our website uses localStorage (not traditional cookies) to store the command count for the live counter feature. This data never leaves your browser and is not accessible to us. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking cookies.

8. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to our processing of your data
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email tonymomoh@icloud.com with the subject line "Data Request".

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal data. Email communications are encrypted in transit. We do not store financial information. If a data breach occurs that is likely to affect your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the ICO as required by law.

10. International Transfers

Our services may involve data being processed by third-party services based outside the UK or EEA (such as GitHub, based in the US). Where this occurs, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Children

ConceptNet is an enterprise software product not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the "last updated" date at the top. For significant changes, we will notify users who have signed up for updates.
